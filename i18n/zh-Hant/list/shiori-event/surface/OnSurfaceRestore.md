# OnSurfaceRestore

**カテゴリ:** Surface event

Occurs once after a certain time has passed since the talk.<br />
In SSP, it occurs the number of seconds configured as the talk timeout in the baseware settings plus 15 seconds later (that is, 15 seconds after the balloon closes).<br />
※The balloon remains displayed until OnSurfaceRestore occurs only when the talk timeout setting is 0.

- **Reference0**: Current surface on the main character side.
- **Reference1**: Current surface on the partner side.

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW / Ninix
