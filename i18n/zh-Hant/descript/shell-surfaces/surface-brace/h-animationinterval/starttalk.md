# starttalk

**カテゴリ:** surface brace \> animation interval

Runs when a talk for the relevant scope begins.<br />
Specifically, it runs when text starts being spoken in a balloon. (This includes synchronized display using the `\_s` tag.)<br />
Note that in scripts where the scope switches frequently over a short period, the animation may not keep up and may not run.

**対応:** SSP 2.7.26
