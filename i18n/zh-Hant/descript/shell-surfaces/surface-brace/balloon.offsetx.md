# balloon.offsetx, coordinates

**カテゴリ:** surface brace

The X coordinate for the balloon position on this surface only (for either the main character or the partner).<br />
Can also be written as offset.x.<br />
For the format that appends a single character indicating the direction (l / r / c / b), see [sakura.balloon.offsetx](sakura.balloon.offsetx.md).

**既定値:** When displayed to the left or right: the position where the balloon's left or right edge aligns with the surface's right or left edge<br />
When displayed above the head or at the feet: the center of the surface

**対応:** SSP
