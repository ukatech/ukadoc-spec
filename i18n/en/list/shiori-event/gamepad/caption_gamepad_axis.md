# Axis name list

- **LeftStickX**: Left stick horizontal (right is positive, left is negative). Normalized range: -1.0 to 1.0.
- **LeftStickY**: Left stick vertical (up is positive, down is negative). Normalized range: -1.0 to 1.0.
- **RightStickX**: Right stick horizontal (right is positive, left is negative). Normalized range: -1.0 to 1.0.
- **RightStickY**: Right stick vertical (up is positive, down is negative). Normalized range: -1.0 to 1.0.
- **LeftTrigger**: Left trigger (value increases as it is pulled). Normalized range: 0.0 to 1.0.
- **RightTrigger**: Right trigger (value increases as it is pulled). Normalized range: 0.0 to 1.0.
