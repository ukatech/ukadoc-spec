# char\*.defaultleft

**カテゴリ:** Ghost information

The X coordinate of the default position of the \*th character (`\p[*]`). The format and meaning are the same as [sakura.defaultleft](sakura.defaultleft.md).<br />
char0 and char1 are used when sakura and kero are not specified.

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW
