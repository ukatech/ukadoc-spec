# Notes

※Information about tea party version support

- **Supported**: The ghost's version is the same as or higher than the version of “Sounds of Everyday Life in Melody,” so it fully supports the tea party events provided by “Sounds of Everyday Life in Melody.”
- **Not supported**: The ghost's version is lower than the version of “Sounds of Everyday Life in Melody,” so it cannot process tea party events from versions higher than its own.
- **Automatic support**: The ghost's version is lower than the version of “Sounds of Everyday Life in Melody,” but it can automatically support tea party events from higher versions by changing the conversation, for example by substituting words.
- **Fixed support**: Support does not vary according to the tea party content provided by “Sounds of Everyday Life in Melody.” Reference0 of the response event is the tea party version verified by the ghost's creator. Ghosts that send this support information do not take the selected tea party content into account.
