# OnJitenTagBattle

**カテゴリ:** Ghost "Ghost Dictionary"

Occurs on every turn of a mock battle fought by the ghost.

- **Reference0**: The sending ghost's `\0` name.
- **Reference1**: Attacking character's name.
- **Reference2**: Defending character's name.
- **Reference3**: Battle result.<br />
  At start: Mock battle started<br />
  During action: (Physical attack successful|Physical attack failed|Physical attack evaded|Physical attack missed|Mental attack successful|Mental attack failed|Mental attack evaded|Mental attack missed)
- **Reference4**: Damage.
- **Reference5**: Attacking character's LIFE.
- **Reference6**: Defending character's LIFE.
- **Reference7**: Tag
