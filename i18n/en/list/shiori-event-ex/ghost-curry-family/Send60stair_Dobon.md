# Send60stair_Dobon

**カテゴリ:** Ghost "Curry Family / Saki and Ukon"

Occurs when any participating ghost gets a Dobon during a game played by Curry Family / Saki and Ukon.

- **Reference0**: Name of the ghost that got a Dobon.
- **Reference1**: Current position of the ghost that got a Dobon.
- **Reference2**: Marked position of the ghost that got a Dobon.
- **Reference3**: Result of the die.
