# OnKinokoObjectChanging

**カテゴリ:** App “Kinoko”

Occurs when a Kinoko skin is about to be switched.

- **Reference0**: Name of the Kinoko skin before switching.
- **Reference1**: Names of the ghosts primarily targeted by the Kinoko skin before switching. If there are multiple names, they are comma-separated.
- **Reference2**: Category of the Kinoko skin before switching. If there are multiple categories, they are comma-separated.
