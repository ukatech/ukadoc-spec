# OnKinokoObjectCreate

**カテゴリ:** App “Kinoko”

Occurs when a Kinoko skin appears on the head.

- **Reference0**: Name of the Kinoko skin.
- **Reference1**: Names of the ghosts primarily targeted by the Kinoko skin. If there are multiple names, they are comma-separated.
- **Reference2**: Category of the Kinoko skin. If there are multiple categories, they are comma-separated.
