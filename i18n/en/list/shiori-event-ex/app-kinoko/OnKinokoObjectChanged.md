# OnKinokoObjectChanged

**カテゴリ:** App “Kinoko”

Occurs when the Kinoko skin is switched.

- **Reference0**: Name of the Kinoko skin after switching.
- **Reference1**: Names of the ghosts primarily targeted by the Kinoko skin after switching. If there are multiple names, they are comma-separated.
- **Reference2**: Category of the Kinoko skin after switching. If there are multiple categories, they are comma-separated.
