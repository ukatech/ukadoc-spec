# Notes

## Surface position information

- **top**: Display attached to the top.
- **bottom**: Display attached to the bottom.
- **free**: Move freely.
- **osuwari,options,…**: \[SSP 2.9.33\] Follow the rectangle of another window or the screen (sit-down behavior). List the options as comma-separated `--name=value` entries, as with [`\![set,alignmenttodesktop,osuwari,…]`](../../../list/sakura-script/surface/bang-set-alignmenttodesktop.note.md). Example: `sakura.seriko.alignmenttodesktop,osuwari,--target=active,--origin=tr,--x=-20,--y=-90,--xmove,--area=workarea,--side=bottom`
