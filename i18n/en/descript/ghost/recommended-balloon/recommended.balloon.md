# recommended.balloon,balloon name

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt - Recommended balloon

The name of the recommended balloon for use with this ghost. A strong warning is displayed when switching to another balloon.<br />
\[SSP 2.9.35\] The balloon's [UUID](../../../list/propertysystem/property-system/general-property/uuid.md) can be specified instead of its name. The warning displayed when switching balloons is also determined by the specified UUID.

**既定値:** Not specified

**対応:** SSP 2.4
