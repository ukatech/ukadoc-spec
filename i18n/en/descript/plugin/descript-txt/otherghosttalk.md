# otherghosttalk,Option

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt

Configures whether to notify the PLUGIN Event [OnOtherGhostTalk](../../../list/plugin-event/plugin-event/OnOtherGhostTalk.md).

- **false**: Do not notify
- **0**: Do not notify
- **true**: Notify immediately afterward (immediately after speaking finishes)
- **1**: Notify immediately afterward
- **after**: Notify immediately afterward
- **before**: Notify immediately beforehand (just before speaking)

**既定値:** false

**対応:** SSP
