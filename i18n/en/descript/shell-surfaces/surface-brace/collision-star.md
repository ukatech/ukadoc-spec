# collision\*,StartX,StartY,EndX,EndY,ID

**カテゴリ:** surface directive

A hit-test area. The enclosed area becomes the region for the ID.<br />
The \* portion (collisionID) is a sequential number that must be unique within the same surface.<br />
See [collision-sort](../descript-brace/collision-sort.md) for the relationship between overlapping hit-test areas and ID order.

**既定値:** No effect

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW
