# point.osuwaripos.x,coordinates

**カテゴリ:** surface brace

For sitting (the `--origin=osuwari` option of [`\![set,alignmenttodesktop,osuwari,…]`](../../../list/sakura-script/surface/bang-set-alignmenttodesktop.note.md)), the X coordinate of the point aligned with another window or the screen rectangle. This is the distance from the left edge of the surface and scales with the surface.

**既定値:** Same as `point.basepos.x` (if not specified, the default is the center of the surface (width ÷ 2))

**対応:** SSP (2.9.34)
