# Assigning Surface IDs

The assignment of surface IDs to particular expressions or content is basically unrestricted.

However, surface0 and surface10 are required as the default surfaces, respectively. Even a ghost without a counterpart must provide a transparent image or similar for surface10 (SSP does not strictly require this; the default surfaces can also be changed through settings such as those in descript).

The following is a rough "de facto standard" for ID assignment. The assignments below are for reference only and are not required, except for 0 and 10; assignments are generally unrestricted.

sakura side

- 00 Neutral
- 01 Blushing
- 02 Surprised
- 03 Worried
- 04 Depressed
- 05 Smiling
- 06 Eyes closed
- 07 Angry
- 08 Sneering
- 09 Blushing and angry
- 25 Singing

kero side

- 10 Neutral
- 11 Wide-eyed
- 19 Singing

Note that in MATERIA, the numbers usable for surfaceID are limited to 0 through 8192.
