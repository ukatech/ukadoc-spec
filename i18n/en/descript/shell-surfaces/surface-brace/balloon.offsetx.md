# balloon.offsetx, coordinates

**カテゴリ:** surface brace

The X coordinate of the balloon position for this surface only (for either the main character or the partner).<br />
Can also be written as offset.x.<br />
For a format with a single direction character (l / r / c / b) appended, see [sakura.balloon.offsetx](sakura.balloon.offsetx.md).

**既定値:** When displayed to the left or right: a position where the left or right edge of the balloon aligns with the right or left edge of the surface<br />
When displayed above or below: the center of the surface

**対応:** SSP
