# parallelstart,(ID1,ID2...)

**カテゴリ:** surface brace \> Drawing Methods

Plays all animations for the specified IDs (the numbers in place of \* in animation\*).<br />
The surface ID, weight, and XY coordinates are ignored in a pattern definition that specifies this drawing method.<br />
In SSP, square brackets \[\] and a period "." instead of a comma "," as a separator are also accepted.<br />
Cannot be used in dressup or elements.

**対応:** SSP
