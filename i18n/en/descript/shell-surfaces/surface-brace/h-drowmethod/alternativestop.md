# alternativestop,(ID1,ID2...)

**カテゴリ:** surface breath \> drawing method

Stop the animation for any one of the specified IDs (the numbers for animation\*'s \*).<br />
In pattern definitions that specify this drawing method, the surface ID, weight, and XY coordinates are ignored.<br />
In SSP, this is also loaded when the brackets are \[\] and the separator is "." instead of ",".<br />
Cannot be used with dressup or element.

**対応:** SSP
