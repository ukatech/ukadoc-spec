# replace

**カテゴリ:** surface brace \> Drawing Methods

Replaces part of the *base layer* with the *new layer*.<br />
Even transparent-color areas and semitransparent areas with transparency information overwrite the *base layer* without preserving its information.<br />
Areas outside the *new layer* are empty areas, not areas with 100% transparency. No compositing occurs in these areas, and the drawing of the *base layer* simply remains unchanged.<br />
Can also be used in dressup and elements.

**対応:** SSP
