# interpolate

**カテゴリ:** surface brace \> Drawing methods

Overlay the *new layer* according to the transparency of the *base layer*.<br />
In semitransparent areas of the *base layer*, the more transparent a part is, the more strongly the *new layer* is composited; fully transparent areas composite it completely, while opaque areas do not composite it at all.<br />
This method is the counterpart to the [overlayfast](overlayfast.md) method.<br />
This can also be used with dressup and element.

**対応:** SSP
