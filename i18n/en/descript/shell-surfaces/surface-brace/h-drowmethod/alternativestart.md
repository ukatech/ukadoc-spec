# alternativestart,(ID1,ID2...)

**カテゴリ:** surface breath \> Drawing method

Plays the animation of one of the specified IDs (the number in animation\*).\ <br />
In pattern definitions that specify this drawing method, the surface ID, weight, and XY coordinates are ignored.<br />
In SSP, it is also read when parentheses are \[\] and the separator is "." instead of ",".<br />
