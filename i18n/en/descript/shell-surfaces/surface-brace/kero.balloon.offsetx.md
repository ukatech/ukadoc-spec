# kero.balloon.offsetx,coordinates

**カテゴリ:** surface brace

The X coordinate of the partner's balloon position for this surface only.<br />
For syntax that appends one character indicating direction (l / r / c / b), see [sakura.balloon.offsetx](sakura.balloon.offsetx.md).

**既定値:** When displayed on the left or right: position where the balloon's left or right edge aligns with the surface's right or left edge<br />
When displayed above the head or at the feet: center of the surface

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW
