# animation\*.collision\*, collision definition animation\*.collisionex\*, collision definition (ex)

**カテゴリ:** surface brace

Definitions that apply only while an animation is running, for regular [collision](collision-star.md) and [collisionex](collisionex-star.md). These are also effective for interval binds.<br />
The \* following animation is the animationID (the same as the \* in animation\*.interval).<br />
The \* following collision is a number that does not overlap with any other number in the series of animation\*.collision definitions.

**既定値:** No behavior

**対応:** SSP
