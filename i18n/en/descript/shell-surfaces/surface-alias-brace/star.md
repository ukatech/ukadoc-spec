# \*,\[\*\]

**カテゴリ:** surface.alias brace

Assigns arbitrary alias IDs to surface IDs.<br />
For example, setting 驚き,\[2\] makes `\s[驚き]` mean the same as `\s[2]`.<br />
You can also specify multiple surfaces separated by commas, such as 1,\[1,11,21\],<br />
in which case `\s[1]` randomly selects from 1, 11, and 21.

**既定値:** No behavior

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW
