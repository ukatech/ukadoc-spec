# option,option 1,option 2,...

**カテゴリ:** surfacetable.txt

Specifies options. Multiple options can be listed, separated by commas.<br />
Currently, only "DisableNoDefineSurfaces" can be specified. It ignores surfaces that have no definition in files such as surfaces.txt and are not explicitly listed in surfacetable.txt.

**既定値:** No behavior

**対応:** SSP
