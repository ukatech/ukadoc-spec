# group,group name

**カテゴリ:** surfacetable.txt

Assigns an identifying name to a group of [surface name](ID.md) definition lines described below.<br />
Follow it immediately with a line containing only "\{" and close it with a line containing only "\}".<br />
The group name "\_\_disabled" indicates that the surfaces in the group are unused (they will no longer appear in SSP's Surface Test list).

**既定値:** No behavior

**対応:** SSP
