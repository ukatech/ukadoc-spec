# char\*.bindgroup\*.addid,ID

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt

Configures dressup co-activation. When the dressup for animation ID \* becomes active (visible), the dressup specified as addid is also activated. Multiple values can be specified, separated by commas.<br />
A co-activated dressup is deactivated when the dressup that activated it is deactivated. If the same dressup is specified for co-activation by multiple dressups, it remains active until all of those dressups are deactivated. (Third character and later)

**既定値:** No behavior

**対応:** SSP
