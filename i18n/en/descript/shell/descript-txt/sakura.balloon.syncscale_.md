# sakura.balloon.syncscale,true

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt

Forcibly enables synchronization of the baseware-side balloon's scaling with the ghost (only true is effective).<br />
Do not overuse this, as it interferes with user interaction.

**既定値:** ghost's descript settings (balloon.syncscale)

**対応:** SSP 2.7 RC1
