# number.y,coordinate [\*1](../index.md#balloon設定)

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt

Y coordinate of the counter number.<br />
In horizontal writing ([vertical](vertical.md) is 0), this indicates the top edge of the number. In vertical writing, it indicates the bottom edge (vertical numbers extend upward from the bottom edge).

**既定値:** Unknown

**対応:** Materia / SSP / CROW
