# otherghosttalk,Option

**カテゴリ:** descript.txt

Setting for notifying the PLUGIN Event [OnOtherGhostTalk](../../../list/plugin-event/plugin-event/OnOtherGhostTalk.md).

- **false**: Do not notify
- **0**: Do not notify
- **true**: Notify immediately after the ghost finishes speaking
- **1**: Notify immediately after
- **after**: Notify immediately after
- **before**: Notify immediately before the ghost speaks

**既定値:** false

**対応:** SSP
